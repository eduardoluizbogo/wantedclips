Privacy Policy

Wanted Clips · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what data Wanted Clips handles when a team member connects a TikTok account and how it is used.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic account information (open ID, display name and avatar), to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, to upload videos on the account's behalf.
  • Upload and publishing status of the videos we send, and the privacy options the account allows.

We do not read followers, messages, likes, comments, viewing history or any other account data.

How we use it

Only to upload videos that the team approved to the connected account and to confirm that the upload worked. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and publishing records are stored only on the team's own computer, in files that are not shared. We do not sell or share this data with anyone. It is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. To delete it, revoke Wanted Clips's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) or contact us, and we delete the stored tokens and records.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is used only by the Wanted Clips team.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
